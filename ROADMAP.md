# Roadmap

## Current foundation

**Being qualified:**
- repository identity and legal texts;
- architecture and governance;
- native documentation;
- dedicated validation tools.

**Domain capabilities and claims:** empty.

## Capability milestones

1. **The four v1 record schemas with golden fixtures** (`facility_readiness_record`, `technology_profile`, `prototyping_board_profile`, `leaf_cell_binding_map`), enrolling full strict quality gates in the same change.
2. **Open-PDK technology profiles** (`IHP SG13G2`, `Sky130`, `GF180MCU` and predictive `ASAP7`) with pinned errata and four-class leaf-cell maps.
3. **The local `PYNQ-Z2` (`xc7z020clg400-1`) prototyping board profile** for built-stage synthesis and place-and-route (`70 %` utilisation cap, positive slack and enumerated configuration surfaces).
4. **Target-descriptor conformance alignment** with Evidence Manifest Toolkit.
5. **Generic leaf and board profile migration** from SC-NeuroCore once split gates pass.

**Enrolled with every implementation change:**
- strict native typing and linting;
- native documentation;
- meaningful statement and branch coverage;
- affected boundary tests and benchmarks.

**Requires its own evidence and explicit approval:**
- admitting any proprietary EDA or foundry licence;
- attaching and probing a physical prototyping board;
- accepting any external NDA material;
- public activation and release.

No availability date is promised. No performance, yield or productivity gain is promised either.
